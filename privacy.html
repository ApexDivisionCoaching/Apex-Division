The Short Version

  • We collect what we need to coach you: your contact details, goals, training and health information, progress check-ins, and payment records.
  • We use it only to run your coaching, handle billing, and talk with you. We never sell your information and we don't run ads with it.
  • Your health information and progress photos stay private. We never post them publicly without your separate written permission.
  • A few trusted services help us run the business (payments, email, scheduling). They only get what they need to do that job.
  • You can ask to see, fix or delete your information at any time by emailing contact@apexdivisioncoaching.com.

This summary is for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.

01Who We Are

Apex Division LLC ("Apex Division," "we," "us") is a Louisiana online fitness coaching business. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit apexdivisioncoaching.com, take our quiz, book a call, enroll, buy a program, join the Apex Collective, or work with us as a coaching client. It works alongside our Terms of Service and Coaching Agreement.

02What We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email, phone number, city and state, time zone, and social media handle if you share one.
  • Coaching intake: date of birth, goals, training experience and schedule, body measurements such as height and weight, nutrition habits, sleep and stress levels.
  • Health screening: answers to our readiness questions, injuries, medications, health conditions, and an emergency contact. See Health Information below.
  • Coaching check-ins: progress photos, measurements, training logs, form-check videos, and messages you send your coach.
  • Agreement records: your typed signature, the date and time you signed, the agreement version, and the type of device and browser you used.
  • Quiz answers and call bookings: what you choose in our plan quiz and what you share when booking a call.

Information from payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive your name, email, what you bought, and the payment status. We never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and content providers receive basic technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. We do not currently use advertising or analytics tracking on our site.

03Health Information

Some of what you share is sensitive, like health conditions, medications, injuries, pregnancy, and progress photos. We treat it with extra care:

  • We use it only to keep your training safe and to build and adjust your plan.
  • Only Apex Division coaches, and the services that deliver our forms and email, can access it.
  • We never sell it, use it for advertising, or share your photos, name or results publicly without your separate written permission.
  • Apex Division is a coaching business, not a healthcare provider, so HIPAA does not apply to us. We still protect your information as described in this policy.

04How We Use Your Information

  • To review your intake, recommend a plan, and decide whether coaching is safe for you or whether you need a doctor's clearance first.
  • To build, deliver and adjust your training and nutrition programs and respond to your check-ins.
  • To process payments, send receipts, and handle cancellations and refunds.
  • To contact you about your coaching, your account, and billing by email, text or the method you chose.
  • To send occasional updates or offers, only if you agree to them; you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep records of your signed agreement, and to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To protect our clients, coaches and business, for example to prevent fraud or misuse of our materials.

05Who We Share It With

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Stripe: payment processing.
  • FormSubmit: delivers enrollment forms to our business email.
  • ZenBusiness and its email provider: our business email and domain.
  • Calendly: booking free discovery calls.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts our website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: load fonts and exercise images on our pages.
  • Discord, Zoom or similar tools: group chat and video calls, if you take part.

These services have their own privacy policies. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety in an emergency, or as part of a sale or merger of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

06Cookies & Browser Storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our site saves a few things in your own browser so it works smoothly: your Form Library access code, so you don't have to re-enter it, your quiz result, and your enrollment progress, so you can pick up where you left off. These stay on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them. Services such as Calendly and Stripe may set their own cookies when you use them.

07How Long We Keep It

  • Coaching information (intake, check-ins, progress photos): while you're a client, and up to 2 years after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Signed agreements and payment records: as long as needed for legal, tax and dispute purposes, typically up to 7 years.
  • Enrollments that never became clients: deleted within 12 months.

08Your Choices & Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you, and send you a copy.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information, including progress photos, except records we're legally required to keep.
  • Stop sending you marketing messages.
  • Withdraw permission you gave us to share your results or photos.

Email contact@apexdivisioncoaching.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws, and we'll honor them.

09Security

We use reputable providers, secure (HTTPS) connections, strong passwords and two-step verification on our business accounts, and we limit access to coaches who need it. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach ever affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

10Age Requirement

Our services are for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our business or the law changes. We'll change the effective date at the top, and for meaningful changes we'll tell active clients by email.
