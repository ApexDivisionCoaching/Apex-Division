FAQ
COMMON
QUESTIONS.
Do I need to be advanced to work with Apex Division? +
Not at all. Our programs and coaching are built for beginners through advanced athletes. The discovery call exists specifically to figure out where you are and what you need. If you're brand new we'll build your foundation the right way. If you're advanced we'll take you further than you've been.
What equipment do I need? +
For the powerlifting and bodybuilding programs you need access to a commercial gym or a home gym with a barbell, squat rack, and plates. The fat loss program can be adapted to most gym setups. During your discovery call we'll factor in exactly what you have access to.
How is online coaching different from just using an AI or following YouTube? +
AI can write a workout. YouTube has free tutorials. What neither can do is notice when you've gone quiet for a week and follow up. Watch your form video and tell you exactly why your squat depth is inconsistent. Adjust your program at week 5 because your recovery isn't keeping up. Build a relationship where you actually feel accountable to someone who cares whether you show up. That's the difference.
What if I have injuries or physical limitations? +
Tell us on the discovery call and we build around it. Every program is customized — we're not handing you a template. Injury history, mobility limitations, equipment restrictions — all of it gets factored in before your first session.
Can I cancel my coaching subscription? +
Yes. All monthly coaching packages can be cancelled at any time. There are no long-term contracts on any coaching tier. The 12 Week Transformation is a one-time payment and runs for its full duration by design.
Which package is right for me? +
That's exactly what the free discovery call is for. Book 15 minutes with us, tell us your goal, your schedule, your history, and your budget — and we'll tell you exactly which package fits. No obligation to buy anything on the call.
How quickly will I see results? +
Strength improvements typically show within 2 to 4 weeks. Body composition changes become visible around weeks 6 to 8 with consistent effort and nutrition. The clients who see the best results are the ones who follow the program, hit their nutrition, and trust the process when it feels slow. We'll be honest with you about realistic timelines for your specific goal during the discovery call.
